# DET Reading Content Specification

## Editorial Direction

DET books should use contemporary, everyday English. Preferred themes include school, family, work, travel, friendships, routines, technology, health, hobbies, and light social topics.

Preferred formats:

- contemporary fiction;
- young adult fiction;
- light mystery and adventure;
- personal stories and diaries;
- accessible nonfiction.

Avoid archaic language, highly literary prose, dense historical context, and passages whose meaning depends on specialist knowledge.

## Content Metadata

Every book should have:

- title and author;
- source and rights status;
- level: A2, B1, or B2;
- genre and topics;
- estimated word count;
- chapter count;
- publication status;
- editorial notes.

## Processing Pipeline

`full text -> chapters -> sentences -> semantic chunks -> questions -> review -> publish`

Sentence boundaries must be preserved. Chunks should end at a natural pause and must not break a key exchange, explanation, or event. Each chunk stores sentence order and word count.

## Rights

MVP content must be public domain, original, licensed, or otherwise explicitly approved for use. Rights status is required before publication.

## Quality Gate

An administrator must verify level, naturalness, chunk boundaries, vocabulary, and question quality before publishing.
